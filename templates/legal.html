Privacy Policy

Vignan University · CSE — Scholarship Agent (Agent 42). Last updated September 2026.

This service helps the university's scholarship section match students to the scholarships they are eligible for, track applications, monitor renewals, and reconcile disbursements against fees. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Vignan University (Computer Science & Engineering). Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to scholarship@vignan.edu.

What data we use

  • Student master data: name, roll number, programme, year, social category and family income (used only for statutory scholarship eligibility, never as a machine-learning feature).
  • Academic and attendance records (CGPA, backlogs, attendance percentage).
  • Scholarship scheme catalogues, application status, sanctions and disbursements.
  • Fee ledger entries and reminder status.

This data originates in the university's own systems (ERP, examinations, attendance, finance). The service reads from that institutional database; it does not collect data from the public.

How we use it

Solely to operate the scholarship workflow described above. Every eligibility decision and every metric is computed deterministically from these records, and the reasoning is shown. The agent's outputs are advisory: actions that affect a student (notifications, reminder suppression) require a human approval before they take effect.

AI assistant. The optional chat assistant sends your question and the relevant scholarship/eligibility figures to Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Gemini) to phrase a reply. The underlying numbers are always computed by this service, not by the model. If the AI is disabled, the assistant answers from built-in templates and no data leaves the service.

Cookies & local storage

We use a single sign-in session cookie and store small preferences (theme, cookie consent) in your browser. See the Cookie Policy. There is no third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

Retention & your rights

Records are retained per the university's academic and financial record-keeping policies. As a student or staff member you may request access to, or correction of, your data through the scholarship section.

Cookie Policy

This service uses the minimum browser storage needed to work. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What we store

  • Session cookie (essential). Keeps you signed in as you move between pages. It is removed when you sign out or the session ends.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. Clearing the session cookie will sign you out; clearing preferences resets the theme and the cookie notice.
